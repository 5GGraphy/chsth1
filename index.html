Clinical Experience

Career Support

Admission Process

Follow these simple steps to begin your journey at the College of Health Sciences and Technology, Hadejia.

1

Check Eligibility

Ensure you meet the admission requirements including O'Level credits in key science subjects (Biology, Chemistry, English).

2

Submit Application

Complete the online application form and upload all required documents through our secure application portal.

3

Entrance Examination

Take the computer-based entrance examination at the scheduled date and time at our examination center.

4

Receive Admission

Check your admission status online and complete registration procedures if offered admission to begin your journey.
